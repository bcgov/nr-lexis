# 6. Who can do what

Your role decides which menu items, records and buttons you see. Industry
users hold the Provincial Submitter role.

## Your role

| Role | For | Lets you |
|---|---|---|
| Provincial Submitter | Companies that apply for exemptions and permits or make offers, and their agents. Granted for each forest client you act for. | Enter and upload applications, add packages, summary of scale and documents, place and withdraw offers, see your exemptions, request permits, send them for review and print them. |

## What a Provincial Submitter sees

You work under one forest client at a time (see [Choosing an
organization](01-getting-started.md#choosing-an-organization)). Under that
client you see:

| Record | You see |
|---|---|
| Applications | Those where your organization is the owner or the agent. |
| Offers | Those your organization placed, and those placed on your applications. |
| Exemptions | Those linked to your applications, and every Blanket OIC exemption. |
| Permits | Those where your organization is the owner or the agent. |

As an agent you can name any forest client as the owner on an application
or a Blanket OIC permit, as long as your own organization is the agent.

## What ministry staff do

These steps are done by staff in the regional export offices, not by you:

- approving, rejecting or withdrawing an application for the Advertising
  List;
- recording the review and decision on an offer;
- creating and approving exemptions;
- confirming permit fees, recording payment, completing or cancelling a
  permit;
- withdrawing an offer after the withdrawal deadline;
- changing an application after its list date.

## Getting a role

Roles are granted by the ministry. See [Getting
access](01-getting-started.md#getting-access). If a menu item or button
described in this manual is missing for you, or you need access for another
forest client, contact your regional export office.
