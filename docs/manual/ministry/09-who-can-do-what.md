# 9. Who can do what

Your role decides which menu items, records and buttons you see. Roles are
granted in FAM (Forest Access Management). Read Only, Application Approver
and Exemption Approver are granted for one or more Natural Resource Regions.
Administrator is province-wide.

## Staff roles

| Role | Lets you |
|---|---|
| Read Only | Find and open applications, offers, exemptions, permits and federal applications. Read application remarks. Run reports. Print completed permits. |
| Application Approver | Everything Read Only can, plus: review applications for the Advertising List; enter, upload and change applications; enter offers and record their decisions; create exemptions and manage their applications; start, review, complete, cancel and reactivate permits; override fees; add remarks to federal applications and reject or withdraw them; add and delete documents. |
| Exemption Approver | Find, change and approve Ministerial exemptions, open the applications linked to them, and correct those applications' packages and scale while the exemption has volume remaining. Nothing else. |
| Administrator | Everything an Application Approver and an Exemption Approver can, in every region, plus the **Admin** menu, publishing notifications and **Print approved exemption**. |

Application Approver and Exemption Approver can't be held together. An
account granted both gets no access until one is removed. Read Only can be
held with one approver role.

## Regions

The eight Natural Resource Regions are Cariboo, Kootenay-Boundary,
Northeast, Omineca, Skeena, South Coast, Thompson-Okanagan and West Coast.

- Searches, review queues and report options hold your regions only.
- You can change or approve a record only in a region where your role
  allows it. A record outside your regions opens for reading, if it opens
  at all.
- A record that covers several regions needs all of them. For example, an
  exemption with applications in Cariboo and Skeena can be changed or
  approved only by someone granted both.
- Each role keeps its own regions. With Read Only for all eight regions and
  Application Approver for Cariboo, you can read everywhere and change
  records in Cariboo.
- A record with no current region can be opened only by an Administrator.

## Industry roles

Industry users log in with a Business BCeID and hold one of these roles.

| Role | Lets them |
|---|---|
| Provincial Submitter | Work under one forest client at a time: enter and upload applications, place and withdraw offers, see their exemptions, request permits and send them for review, print completed permits. They see records where that client is the owner or the agent, offers that client placed, and every Blanket OIC exemption. |
| Federal Read Only | Find and read every federal application. |

## Getting a role

Roles and regions are assigned in FAM by the people who administer LEXIS
access. If a menu item or button described in this manual is missing for
you, ask them to check your role and regions.
